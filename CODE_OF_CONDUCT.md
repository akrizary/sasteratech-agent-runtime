# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SasteraTech Agent Runtime project spaces should support respectful, evidence-based, and
constructive collaboration. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and defects without attacking people;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- protect credentials, personal information, and confidential reports; and
- correct mistakes transparently without harassment or retaliation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not accepted in project-controlled spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualised conduct;
- insults, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring someone to disclose credentials or confidential material;
- knowingly impersonating the official project or another contributor; or
- retaliating against a good-faith safety, security, or conduct report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions,
official community channels, events, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Conduct and security reports must use a private route. The first public release
is blocked until that route is configured in `SECURITY.md`.

Depending on severity and evidence, maintainers may provide a private warning,
remove content, restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant
from project-controlled spaces. Immediate restrictions may be used when needed
to protect people, credentials, or confidential reports. Decisions should be
documented privately, limited to the relevant scope, and reviewed for conflicts
of interest where practical.

This project-authored policy may be revised as the community and governance
model mature.
