# SasteraTech Agent Runtime Brand and Trademark Policy

## Current legal status

`SasteraTech Runtime`, `SasteraTech Agent Runtime`, and the associated project logo are project
brand identifiers claimed by Mohammad Akri bin Eddy Akhyar, the current
SasteraTech Agent Runtime project owner, through use.
They are **not represented as registered trademarks** and no MyIPO registration
is claimed by this document.

Do not use the registered-trademark symbol (`®`) with these identifiers unless
and until the relevant mark has actually been registered for the applicable
goods or services and territory.

trademarks, service marks, or product names except for reasonable and customary

The applicable licence text is in `LICENSE`; third-party material remains under
the licences identified in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Uses that do not require separate brand permission

Subject to applicable law, you may:

- refer accurately to the project by name;
- state that independent software is compatible with or based on the official
  SasteraTech Agent Runtime open-source source distribution;
- retain required attribution and notices when redistributing an unmodified
  official release under its applicable source-code licence; and
- link to the official project without implying sponsorship or endorsement.

These uses must be truthful, must not create confusion about origin, and must
not imply that a fork, service, package, or organisation is official.

## Uses requiring permission

Written permission from the project owner is required to:

- use the official logo as the primary identity of another product or service;
- name a modified distribution or hosted service `SasteraTech Runtime`,
  `SasteraTech Agent Runtime`, or a confusingly similar name;
- use the brand in a company name, domain, social-media account, application
  listing, conference, certification, or merchandise; or
- claim endorsement, certification, partnership, or official status.

## Forks and modified distributions

Forks may describe their origin factually, for example:

> Based on the SasteraTech Agent Runtime open-source core; this is an independent fork
> and is not endorsed by the SasteraTech Agent Runtime project.

The fork's product name, primary logo, package identity, and service identity
must be distinguishable from the official project. Required copyright, licence,
and attribution notices remain governed by the applicable source-code licence.

## No transfer through contribution

Submitting code, documentation, designs, or other contributions does not grant
the contributor ownership of the project brand and does not grant the project
rights to a contributor's separate marks beyond what is expressly agreed.

## Contact and future registration

The current copyright owner is identified in `COPYRIGHT.md`. Before public
release, the project owner will identify an official private contact in the
publication metadata. A MyIPO application may be considered later if adoption
or revenue justifies the filing cost.

This policy is a project governance statement, not legal advice. Unregistered
brand rights and remedies depend on applicable law and evidence of actual use.
